## Return window and conditions

Customers are eligible to return purchased items within **30 days** of purchase, provided they present a valid receipt [^internal-policy-store-ops]. 

To qualify for a refund, all returned items must meet the following criteria:
* **Condition:** Items must be completely unused, in their original packaging, and in re-sellable condition [^internal-policy-store-ops].
* **Proof of Purchase:** A valid receipt or order confirmation must be presented [^internal-policy-store-ops].

## Processing timelines

Once a return is inspected and approved at our facility, refunds are processed and typically deposited back to the original payment method within **5 working days** [^internal-policy-store-ops].

## Exceptions for faulty goods

Faulty, damaged, or defective goods are fully exempt from the standard 30-day return window and item condition requirements [^internal-policy-store-ops]. Customers experiencing issues with defective products may report and return them outside the standard timeframe for a full replacement or refund [^internal-policy-store-ops].
